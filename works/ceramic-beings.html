陶與生命容器 Ceramic Beings

陶土在這個系列中不只是器皿，而是與雙手、觸覺、動作與想像一起參與生成的身體。眼睛、嘴巴、孔洞、花與痕跡彼此牽引，讓每件作品在尚未被命名之前，先慢慢長成可以觀看、呼吸與回應的生命。 In this series, clay is not merely a vessel, but a body that participates in emergence together with the hands, touch, movement, and imagination. Eyes, mouths, openings, flowers, and traces draw one another together, allowing each work to slowly grow into a life that can gaze, breathe, and respond before it is named.

心生相 Inner Form

作為《陶與生命容器系列》的第一件作品，《心生相》像是整個系列最初的入口，讓內在形象從陶土、孔洞與器形之間慢慢顯現。 As the first work in Clay and Vessels of Life, Inner Form becomes the series' first threshold, where inner images slowly appear through clay, openings, and vessel-like form.

從器物看見生命如何停駐 Seeing How Life Dwells in Vessels

這個系列可以先從五件代表作品進入：從最初的《心生相》，到拒絕被功能命名的《非花器》，再走向凝視、祭儀與回望。它們讓陶土不只是容器，而是在眼神、呼吸、記憶與雙手的往返之中，逐漸讓生命成形的場所。 The series may be entered through five representative works: from the first Inner Form to Not a Vase, which resists being defined by function, and onward to gaze, ritual, and looking back. Together, they make clay more than a vessel: it becomes a place where life gradually takes form through the interplay of gaze, breath, memory, and the hands.

生命容器的生成 The Formation of Life Vessels

這一段從器形、孔洞與身體開始，讓陶土慢慢脫離單純功能；內在形象不是被安放進容器，而是在陶土、雙手與形體變化之間逐漸顯現。 This section begins with vessel forms, openings, and bodies, allowing clay to gradually move beyond simple function. Inner images are not placed inside a container; they gradually emerge through the interplay of clay, the hands, and changing form.

左足之房右足之屋 House of the Left Foot, Dwelling of the Right

兩件一對，但各自有各自的個性：一隻讓釉自己流成地形，一隻把每一塊顏色圈起來。作者從墨西哥旅行回來之後做的——那趟旅行帶回來的不是圖案，而是「一個物件可以同時是一個人、一間房、一座祭壇」這個想法。 A pair, each with its own character: in one, the glaze is allowed to flow into terrain; in the other, every field of colour is outlined. Made after a journey to Mexico — what the journey brought back was not a motif but an idea: that an object can be a person, a house, and an altar at once.

當容器逐漸接近角色，陶土便不只是承載，而成為可以陪伴、回望與守護的生命身體，讓生命以更親近的姿態慢慢成形。 As the vessel gradually approaches the figure, clay no longer merely holds; it becomes a living body capable of accompanying, looking back, and guarding, allowing life to slowly take form in a more intimate presence.

正面照片 Front 背面照片 Back 另一視角照片 Another view

陶與生命容器，不是把生命放進陶土裡，而是在陶土、雙手、觸覺、動作與想像的往返之中，讓形體在命名之前先慢慢出現。 Ceramic Beings does not place life into clay. Through the movement between clay, hands, touch, gesture, and imagination, form slowly appears before it is named.

創作到後來，我常常不知道雙手最後會帶我走向哪裡；直到形體逐漸生成，我才重新看見其中出現的眼睛、聲音、花、記憶，以及那些尚未被命名的生命。 As the process became freer, I often no longer knew where my hands would lead; only as the form emerged did I begin to see eyes, voices, flowers, memories, and lives that had not yet been named.
