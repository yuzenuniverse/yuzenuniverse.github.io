心象的流動 Inner Worlds in Motion

色彩、記憶與想像在畫面中交會，臉孔、植物與流動的形體彼此轉化，讓內在世界逐漸顯現。 Color, memory, and imagination meet on the canvas; faces, plants, and flowing forms turn into one another, letting an inner world gradually appear.

畫中有如夢境從高空墜落的女人，被鳳鳥所托舉，緩降至海上，乘坐由眼睛化成的小舟，朝出海口前進。兩邊群山充滿著各式各樣的不明生物，注視著這一切，海水中也充滿著奇異的生物，護送著女人前行。整幅畫充滿詭異的氣氛，但還是陽光滿盈。女人張著橘色的小眼睛，靜靜地看著一切的發生，好像已經了生死，無所畏懼。 A woman falls from the sky as if in a dream, caught and lowered to the sea by a phoenix, carried on a small boat made of eyes toward the river mouth. The mountains on either side are full of unknown creatures watching it all, and strange beings in the water escort her onward. The painting is uncanny throughout, yet full of sunlight. Her small orange eyes are open, quietly watching everything happen — as if she has already passed beyond life and death, and is afraid of nothing.

這是一棵能通靈、會思考、會做夢的樹。在夢境裡有眼鏡蛇、老鷹、美麗的少女、貝殼的眼睛，左右各一半側臉、中間有二半側臉，構成整個充滿想像的樹之腦。 A tree that can commune with spirits, that thinks and dreams. Within the dream are a cobra, an eagle, a beautiful young woman, and eyes held in a shell; half a profile on the left, half on the right, and two more halves meeting in the middle — together they form the tree's imagining mind.

被視之流—希望之門 Current of Being Seen - Gate of Hope

系列 Series

收藏 Collection
藝術家自藏 Artist's Collection

「希望之門」是指畫面右上方的小門。大家都想擠進去那道代表希望的窄門——各式各樣的人種、外星人、幽靈，甚至是魚、船上的人、海水中的眾神，象徵我們這個大千世界裡的各種情緒、競爭與關係。尤其是中間由下往上倒掛的神祇，也要伸著長長的象鼻到天上去透氣。整幅畫充滿著怪誕不合理的緊張氣氛，但是顏色還是明亮而有朝氣。 The Gate of Hope is the small door at the upper right. Everyone wants to squeeze through that narrow gate — people of every kind, aliens, ghosts, even fish, the figure in the boat, the deities in the water — standing for all the emotions, rivalries, and relations of this teeming world. Most of all the deity hanging upside down at the centre, stretching a long trunk up to the sky just to breathe. The whole painting is strange, unreasonable, and tense; and the colour stays bright and full of life.

觀看高清原圖 View in High Resolution

這些畫沒有一張是靜止的。洪水、夢境、墜落與托舉——有事情正在發生，而且有先後。顏色始終明亮，即使畫的是不友善的環境。 None of these paintings stands still. A flood, a dream, a falling and a catching — something is happening, and it happens in sequence. The color stays bright, even when what is painted is not a kind world.
