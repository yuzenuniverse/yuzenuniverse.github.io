04.3 / 創作起點 Origins

木之始 Wood Origin

木紋、刀痕與自然形體，是生命宇宙更早顯現的起點。 Wood grain, carved marks, and natural forms as early origins of life.

木紋中的角色與書頁 Figures and Pages in Wood Grain

木紋中的角色與書頁 Figures and Pages in Wood Grain

木材的紋理、板片與可翻閱的結構，使早期角色不只被畫出，也開始具有重量、關係和可以被打開的內部空間。 Wood grain, panels, and book-like structures allow early figures not only to be drawn, but to gain weight, relation, and an inner space that can be opened.

入形之時 Entering Form

作品資訊 Work Details

作品名稱 Title
入形之時 Entering Form
年份 Year
2018
媒材 Medium
樟木浮雕 Camphor wood relief
尺寸 Dimensions
24×26×3cm
系列 Series
木之始系列 Wood Origin Series
收藏 Collection
藝術家自藏 Artist's Collection

角色是臨摹來的，但處理方式已經是作者自己的：把形體嵌進一個挖出來的橢圓凹槽，四周保留樟木的毛邊與粉色木紋。這個「在一塊未整修的木頭上開一個場域，讓生命出現在裡面」的做法，後來在木中精靈與平面陶板裡一再出現。 The figure is copied, but the treatment is already the artist's own: the form is set into a scooped oval recess, with the camphor board's raw edges and pink grain left showing. This device — opening a field in an unfinished piece of wood and letting a life appear inside it — returns again and again in the later wood and clay works.

深色窗紗布包覆書身，粉白與紫紅磁磚錯落其上。細密織紋與光滑釉面相映，讓日常的記錄有了可觸摸的層次。 Dark sheer fabric wraps the body of the book, with pale and wine-red tiles set across it. Fine weave answers glazed surface, giving a record of daily life a texture that can be touched.

觀看高清原圖 View High-Resolution Image

木之始讓木材的重量、紋理與人物寓言成為這些起點裡的一條支線，也讓「觀看自己」第一次具有書頁與物件的形式。 Wood Origin makes the weight, texture, and figure-fables of wood one strand among these origins, giving self-viewing the form of pages and objects for the first time.

這裡的木板像一種安靜的前史，將後來的浮雕、容器與人格結構提前埋入材料之中。 The wood panels here are a quiet prehistory, burying later reliefs, vessels, and persona structures within the material in advance.
